# SkillBridge AI — Smart Career Gap Analyzer

## 🚀 About the Project

SkillBridge AI is an AI-powered web application designed to help students identify skill gaps between their current skills and the requirements of their target job roles.

## 🌐 Live Demo

[Click here to explore SkillBridge AI](https://warping-bridge-career-path.base44.app)

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify skills required for selected career roles.
* Help students understand their skill gaps.
* Provide guidance for career preparation.
* Support students in improving their job readiness.
